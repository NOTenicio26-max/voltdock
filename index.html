05 / Built for smarter mobility

Simple for riders. Intelligent underneath.

VoltDock combines modular hardware with software-led access and inventory management.

ϟ

Quick battery exchange

Compatible, pre-charged battery packs reduce rider downtime compared with waiting for a full charge.

⌑

PIN-linked ownership

A reservation flow can help a rider reclaim their original battery after it has finished charging.

◷

Queue-aware booking

ETA windows and automatic reservation expiry help prevent charged inventory from being held indefinitely.

♻

Battery-aware charging

Battery health, temperature, chemistry, and approved charge limits should guide real charging decisions.
